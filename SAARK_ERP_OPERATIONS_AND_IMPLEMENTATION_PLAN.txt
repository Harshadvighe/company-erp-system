3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE: TWO CORE OPERATIONAL ENGINES
================================================================================

Saark Exploration Pvt. Ltd. operates two primary industrial & trading workflows:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 1: CUSTOM PANEL ASSEMBLY & MANUFACTURING (Engineered-to-Order)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Engineering / NPD:
   - Circuit schematic and pump automation specifications created in NPD.
   - Raw materials and component BOM identified.

2. Material List & Sourcing ("kuthun purchase kelte"):
   - Each component links to vendor purchase records.
   - Displays last supplier name, previous purchase price, and vendor lead time.
   - Auto-categorizes components into:
     * Switchgear (MCB, MCCB, Contactors, OLR)
     * Enclosure & Sheet Metal (IP55 powder-coated box)
     * Busbars & Cabling
     * Control Electronics & Indicators

3. Excel Auto-Quotation Engine:
   - Sales/Engineering staff uploads Excel sheet with BOM lines.
   - System computes:
     Material Cost + Labour & Overheads + Profit Margin (%) + 18% GST (HSN 8537).
   - Generates official Quotation (QUO-2026-XXXXX) in 1 click.

4. Assembly & Quality Control (QC):
   - Shop floor wiring, component mounting, and busbar fitting.
   - Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) protocol verification in /production/management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 2: BMS DIVISION & READY ELECTRONICS TRADING (Make-to-Stock / Trading)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Product Catalog:
   - Standard electronics and IoT products (TP-Link routers, gateways, sensors).
   - Unique SKU codes, electronic specifications, manufacturer warranty.

2. Rapid Desktop Computer Search:
   - Instant search via ERP search bar for instant pricing, specifications, and availability.

3. Live Order Stock Decision ("order madhich decide hoil kiti material ahe"):
   - When entering a Sales Order, system compares order quantity against available warehouse stock.
   - Case A: Stock Sufficient -> Allocated immediately; generates Picking Slip & Tax Invoice.
   - Case B: Stock Shortfall  -> Calculates missing units; automatically generates Purchase 
                                 Requisition to Procurement for vendor order.

4. DETAILED OPERATIONAL & TECHNICAL WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 1: CUSTOM PANEL ASSEMBLY & MANUFACTURING LIFECYCLE (Engineered-to-Order)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Client Enquiry] -> [BOM / Excel Import] -> [Sourcing Match ("kuthun purchase kelte")]
       |
       v
[Auto-Quotation (QUO-2026-XXXXX)] -> [Customer Approval & PO] -> [Sales Order]
       |
       v
[Material Reservation & Shortfall PO] -> [Shop Floor Assembly & Wiring]
       |
       v
[Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT QC)] -> [Dispatch & Tax Invoice]

DETAILED STEP-BY-STEP SOP:
Step 1.1: Customer Enquiry Ingestion
  - Customer provides pump ratings (e.g., 7.5 HP dual-pump booster system) and
    voltage requirements (415V 3-Phase).
  - Sales engineer logs requirement in /#/crm/enquiries.

Step 1.2: Material List (BOM) Ingestion via Excel
  - Engineer compiles or uploads component list (.xlsx) into /#/crm/quotations:
    * Switchgear (MCBs, contactors, overload relays, timers, phase failer).
    * Enclosure (IP55 powder-coated MS box with gland plates).
    * Control & Indication (LED indicators, selector switches, push buttons).
    * Wiring & Busbars (copper busbars, PVC wire ducts, terminal blocks).

Step 1.3: Sourcing Matcher ("kuthun purchase kelte") & Auto-Sorting
  - System checks past InwardEntry and PurchaseOrder records for each item.
  - Automatically identifies:
    * Last Supplier Name (e.g., Schneider Electric distributor, L&T vendor).
    * Last Purchase Unit Price.
    * Supplier Lead Time.
  - Auto-sorts components into standard industrial categories.

Step 1.4: Auto-Quotation Calculation
  - Material Cost = Sum(Quantity * Unit Price).
  - Labour & Assembly Charges = Standardized hourly/rating matrix.
  - Overhead & Testing = Testing fee + packing cost.
  - Margin = Configurable profit percentage (e.g., 15%).
  - Tax = 18% GST under HSN Code 8537 (Electrical Control Panels).
  - Formal PDF Quotation generated in 1 click with standard payment terms.

Step 1.5: Production & FAT Quality Clearance
  - Workstation allocated on shop floor (/production/new).
  - Assembly technicians mount components, wire according to schematic.
  - Quality engineer verifies 7-point FAT protocol in /production/management:
    (1) Visual & dimensional check
    (2) High voltage test (2.5kV for 1 min)
    (3) Insulation resistance (>50 MegaOhms)
    (4) Control logic & trip simulation
    (5) Phase reversal interlock
    (6) Earthing continuity
    (7) Ferruling & cable gland dressing.
  - Inspection certificate signed and panel dispatched.

WORKFLOW 2: BMS DIVISION & READY ELECTRONICS TRADING (Make-to-Stock)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Desktop Computer Search] -> [Sales Order Entry]
                                    |
                                    v
                 [Stock Decision Engine ("order madhich decide hoil")]
                                   / \
                                  /   \
          [Sufficient Stock] <---/     \---> [Stock Shortfall]
                  |                                     |
                  v                                     v
       [Immediate Stock Reserve]              [Auto Purchase Requisition]
                  |                                     |
                  v                                     v
         [Packing & Gate Pass]                  [Vendor Order to Supplier]
                  |                                     |
                  v                                     v
        [Tax Invoice & Dispatch]              [Inward Entry & Dispatch]

DETAILED STEP-BY-STEP SOP:
Step 2.1: Desktop Computer Search (Ctrl + K)
  - Sales executive types product name or model (e.g. "TP-Link", "Gateway", "Sensor").
  - System returns real-time catalog price, technical datasheet, and warehouse stock.

Step 2.2: Sales Order Booking & Real-Time Stock Decision
  - Executive enters order for e.g. 50 units.
  - System immediately triggers Stock Allocation Check:
    * Condition A: In-stock quantity >= 50.
      Result: Badge turns GREEN ("Stock Allocated"). StockItem.availableQuantity
      is instantly reserved to prevent overselling.
    * Condition B: In-stock quantity = 20 (Shortfall = 30).
      Result: Badge turns AMBER/RED ("Shortfall: 30 units").

Step 2.3: Automated Procurement Trigger for Shortfall
  - System prompts: "Generate Purchase Requisition for 30 units?".
  - 1-click creates PurchaseRequisition in /#/purchase/orders linked to this Sales Order.
  - Procurement team places PO with vendor.

Step 2.4: Fast-Track Inward & Customer Dispatch
  - When vendor delivers goods, InwardEntryItem marks them as "Pre-Allocated to Order".
  - System generates Commercial Invoice, E-Way Bill details, and Dispatch Pass.

DETAILED STEP-BY-STEP SOP:

  - Engineer opens /#/production/new and selects "Load from Existing Panel".
  - Chooses from standard templates (e.g., "7.5HP Dual Booster Panel").

  - All existing switchgear, contactors, and relays are populated in the grid.
  - Warehouse stock column shows whether each part is in stock or needs ordering.

Step 3.3: Component Customization ("ani update")
  - Keep standard base (enclosure, terminal blocks, wiring).
  - Swap custom components (e.g., change Siemens VFD to ABB, upgrade 16A MCB to 25A).
  - Update quantities to match new client specifications.

Step 3.4: Instant Cost Recalculation & Save
  - System recalculates total panel cost in real time.
  - Saves as new panel revision (e.g. PANEL-2026-0012) without re-entering unchanged data.

5. STEP-BY-STEP IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
================================================================================

PHASE 1: EXCEL BOM IMPORT & AUTO-QUOTATION GENERATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Eliminate manual quotation calculation; import BOM from Excel and show vendor history.
Steps:
1. Backend Endpoint:
   - POST /api/v1/sales/quotations/import-bom-excel
   - Accepts .xlsx file with columns: Component, Specification, Make, Quantity, Unit.
2. Sourcing Matcher ("kuthun purchase kelte"):
   - Queries historical PurchaseOrder and InwardEntry tables for matching component name.
   - Auto-populates estimated unit price and suggests preferred vendor.
3. Pricing & PDF Output:
   - Automatically generates formal Saark Exploration Quotation with standard terms:
     * Payment: 50% Advance, 50% Against Proforma before Dispatch
     * Delivery: 2 to 3 weeks
     * Warranty: 18 months from supply / 12 months from commissioning

PHASE 2: BMS DIVISION & REAL-TIME ORDER STOCK DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Provide instant inventory decision during order booking.
Steps:
1. Product Master Tagging:
   - Add productType badge: PANEL_ASSEMBLY vs BMS_READY_ELECTRONIC.
2. Sales Order Real-time Check:
   - When user enters product and quantity on Sales Order screen:
   - System displays visual stock badge:
     * GREEN : "Full Stock Available (X units in Main Warehouse)"
     * AMBER : "Partial Stock (X available, Y missing)"
     * RED   : "Zero Stock - Click to create Purchase Requisition"
3. Auto Purchase Requisition:
   - One-click transfer of shortfall to Procurement module.

PHASE 3: DESKTOP SEARCH & EXISTING MATERIAL LIST RE-USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Fast lookup and ability to clone and edit existing BOMs.
Steps:
1. Desktop Search:
   - Keyboard shortcut (Ctrl + K) opens global search for products, parts, and past quotes.
2. BOM Clone & Update ("material ani update"):
   - In /#/production/new, add "Load Existing Panel Template" dropdown.
   - Populates form with existing switchgear specifications.
   - Allows engineer to adjust quantities, swap components, and save as new batch.

PHASE 4: OFFICE EXPENSE (HR / ADMIN) WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Simple management of administrative and operational expenses.
Steps:
1. Expense Entry Screen:
   - Category: Office Stationery, Travel / Conveyance, Tea / Pantry, Utilities, Repairs.
   - Attachment: Receipt / bill photo.
2. Approval Cycle:
   - Submitted by staff -> Approved by Managing Director (Saarkadmin) -> Posted to Accounts ledger.
