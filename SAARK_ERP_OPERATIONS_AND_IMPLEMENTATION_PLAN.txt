3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE: TWO CORE OPERATIONAL ENGINES
================================================================================

Saark Exploration Pvt. Ltd. operates two primary industrial & trading workflows:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 1: CUSTOM PANEL ASSEMBLY & MANUFACTURING (Engineered-to-Order)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Engineering / NPD:
   - Circuit schematic and pump automation specifications created in NPD.
   - Raw materials and component BOM identified.

2. Material List & Sourcing ("kuthun purchase kelte"):
   - Each component links to vendor purchase records.
   - Displays last supplier name, previous purchase price, and vendor lead time.
   - Auto-categorizes components into:
     * Switchgear (MCB, MCCB, Contactors, OLR)
     * Enclosure & Sheet Metal (IP55 powder-coated box)
     * Busbars & Cabling
     * Control Electronics & Indicators

3. Excel Auto-Quotation Engine:
   - Sales/Engineering staff uploads Excel sheet with BOM lines.
   - System computes:
     Material Cost + Labour & Overheads + Profit Margin (%) + 18% GST (HSN 8537).
   - Generates official Quotation (QUO-2026-XXXXX) in 1 click.

4. Assembly & Quality Control (QC):
   - Shop floor wiring, component mounting, and busbar fitting.
   - Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) protocol verification in /production/management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE 2: BMS DIVISION & READY ELECTRONICS TRADING (Make-to-Stock / Trading)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Product Catalog:
   - Standard electronics and IoT products (TP-Link routers, gateways, sensors).
   - Unique SKU codes, electronic specifications, manufacturer warranty.

2. Rapid Desktop Computer Search:
   - Instant search via ERP search bar for instant pricing, specifications, and availability.

3. Live Order Stock Decision ("order madhich decide hoil kiti material ahe"):
   - When entering a Sales Order, system compares order quantity against available warehouse stock.
   - Case A: Stock Sufficient -> Allocated immediately; generates Picking Slip & Tax Invoice.
   - Case B: Stock Shortfall  -> Calculates missing units; automatically generates Purchase 
                                 Requisition to Procurement for vendor order.

4. STEP-BY-STEP IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
================================================================================

PHASE 1: EXCEL BOM IMPORT & AUTO-QUOTATION GENERATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Eliminate manual quotation calculation; import BOM from Excel and show vendor history.
Steps:
1. Backend Endpoint:
   - POST /api/v1/sales/quotations/import-bom-excel
   - Accepts .xlsx file with columns: Component, Specification, Make, Quantity, Unit.
2. Sourcing Matcher ("kuthun purchase kelte"):
   - Queries historical PurchaseOrder and InwardEntry tables for matching component name.
   - Auto-populates estimated unit price and suggests preferred vendor.
3. Pricing & PDF Output:
   - Automatically generates formal Saark Exploration Quotation with standard terms:
     * Payment: 50% Advance, 50% Against Proforma before Dispatch
     * Delivery: 2 to 3 weeks
     * Warranty: 18 months from supply / 12 months from commissioning

PHASE 2: BMS DIVISION & REAL-TIME ORDER STOCK DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Provide instant inventory decision during order booking.
Steps:
1. Product Master Tagging:
   - Add productType badge: PANEL_ASSEMBLY vs BMS_READY_ELECTRONIC.
2. Sales Order Real-time Check:
   - When user enters product and quantity on Sales Order screen:
   - System displays visual stock badge:
     * GREEN : "Full Stock Available (X units in Main Warehouse)"
     * AMBER : "Partial Stock (X available, Y missing)"
     * RED   : "Zero Stock - Click to create Purchase Requisition"
3. Auto Purchase Requisition:
   - One-click transfer of shortfall to Procurement module.

PHASE 3: DESKTOP SEARCH & EXISTING MATERIAL LIST RE-USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Fast lookup and ability to clone and edit existing BOMs.
Steps:
1. Desktop Search:
   - Keyboard shortcut (Ctrl + K) opens global search for products, parts, and past quotes.
2. BOM Clone & Update ("material ani update"):
   - In /#/production/new, add "Load Existing Panel Template" dropdown.
   - Populates form with existing switchgear specifications.
   - Allows engineer to adjust quantities, swap components, and save as new batch.

PHASE 4: OFFICE EXPENSE (HR / ADMIN) WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Simple management of administrative and operational expenses.
Steps:
1. Expense Entry Screen:
   - Category: Office Stationery, Travel / Conveyance, Tea / Pantry, Utilities, Repairs.
   - Attachment: Receipt / bill photo.
2. Approval Cycle:
   - Submitted by staff -> Approved by Managing Director (Saarkadmin) -> Posted to Accounts ledger.
